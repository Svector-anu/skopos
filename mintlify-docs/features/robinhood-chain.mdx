Robinhood Chain (chain 4663) is Robinhood's own L2, where tokenized stocks and memecoins trade onchain. Most routing engines don't cover it — Skopos does, natively, through [Definitive's Flash](https://flash.definitive.fi) for execution and Relay for bridging.

Everything on this page is non-custodial: Skopos quotes, **your wallet signs every step**, and Skopos never touches your funds.

## Native swaps

Same-chain swaps on Robinhood Chain, quoted live through Flash:

```
> swap 10 USDG to ETH on robinhood
> swap 0.01 ETH to CASHCAT on robinhood
```

- **USDG** (Paxos's Global Dollar) is the chain's real stablecoin — there is no meaningful USDC liquidity here. Type USDC anyway and Skopos silently resolves it to USDG.
- Spending native ETH adds an automatic wrap step (the chain's pricing engine trades WETH) — Skopos builds the wrap transaction for you, you just sign it.
- Orders are EIP-712 typed data signed in your own wallet; submission happens server-side, but nothing executes without your signature.

## Buy tokenized stocks

24 stock and ETF tokens — NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META, COIN, SPY, QQQ, and more:

```
> buy $10 of NVDA on robinhood
> buy some TSLA on robinhood chain
```

Ticker resolution goes through **Robinhood's own contract registry first**, never a DEX search. This matters: the chain has heavy ticker-impersonation activity (copycat "AAPL"/"TSLA" tokens with real liquidity of their own), and a symbol search can't tell the real Robinhood-issued token from a fake. The registry can.

## Advanced orders

Limit, stop-loss, take-profit, and TWAP — in plain English, on Robinhood Chain **plus 7 EVM chains** (Base, Arbitrum, Ethereum, Optimism, Polygon, BSC, Avalanche):

```
> buy $2000 of ETH at $1800                      # limit
> sell 2 NVDA if it drops below $400             # stop-loss — yes, on a tokenized stock
> sell 2 ETH when it hits $5000                  # take-profit
> buy $500 of ETH over 7 days                    # TWAP
> sell 2 ETH over 3 days on arbitrum             # TWAP sell, off-Robinhood
```

Add `on base` / `on arbitrum` to place the order there — USDC settles off Robinhood Chain, USDG on it. Natural phrasings the strict patterns miss go through a schema-validated LLM parse that can **never invent a number you didn't type** — a missing price or amount gets asked for, not guessed.

Trigger-order previews show both numbers honestly: what the trigger price implies you'd receive ("est. received at trigger ≈ …, after fees") and what the same sell would fetch **at current price** — clearly labeled so a market quote is never mistaken for trigger proceeds.

## Bridge in and out

Cross-chain routes into chain 4663 aren't covered by the usual aggregators — Skopos bridges through Relay:

```
> bridge 0.05 ETH from base to robinhood chain
> bridge 0.01 ETH from robinhood to base
```

Both directions live-confirmed. This is how funds get onto the chain before trading.

## Launch feed

Roughly one token launches on this chain every 1–2 minutes. Skopos surfaces the feed with real safety signals attached, not just a raw list:

```
> what's launching on robinhood chain
```

Returns the 5 most active recent launches ranked by **volume-to-market-cap ratio** (above 10x gets a 🔥), each with a live risk badge (the same DexScreener liquidity/honeypot scan behind [Token Safety](/features/token-safety)), color-coded liquidity, a repeat-launch warning linking to the deployer's full launch history on NOXA, a copyable contract address, and links to Bankr, DexScreener, and GeckoTerminal.

<Note>
There's no "past day / past week" filter on purpose — the most-recent batch only ever spans about 30 minutes at current launch pace, so a longer window would silently show the same 30 minutes every time. Skopos shows exactly how much time the batch spans instead.
</Note>

A token that's 1–2 minutes old can show "not indexed yet" — that's DexScreener's indexing lag, not a chain-support gap; Skopos says so instead of faking a risk score. And token names on a permissionless chain are unverified — anyone can launch a token riffing on a real person or brand with zero affiliation. Treat the name as decoration; the risk scan and deployer history are the real signals.

## Stock-paired token intel

A Robinhood Chain original: tokens whose liquidity pool quotes against a **tokenized stock** instead of USDG or WETH — $REAL trades against NVDA, so every trade accrues fees *in NVDA* to the creator. Skopos detects the category from pool data and reads it:

```
> price of $REAL vs NVDA          # ratio card — token priced in stock terms
> show stock-paired tokens        # sweep the launch feed for the category
> fee flywheel for $REAL          # est. stock accruing to the creator
```

The card shows the pairing (with a **registry-verified** badge when the quote token matches Robinhood's official contract — an impersonator NVDA pool fails it), the token/stock ratio, the live stock price via Pyth, and the fee flywheel: estimated daily and since-launch creator fees, in USD and stock-token terms.

<Note>
Fee figures are **estimates from trading volume**, based on standard Doppler launch parameters (0.7% pool fee, 95% creator share) — not exact unclaimed balances. Individual tokens can configure different tiers.
</Note>

Research commands (`deep dive on 0x…`, `is X safe`, `research X`) automatically attach the stock-pairing context when the token qualifies.
