## Read odds

```
> what are people betting on
> odds eth hits $5k this year
> top prediction markets
```

Returns live markets and odds from [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com), optionally scoped to a topic you name.

## Deposit

```
> deposit into polymarket
```

Skopos can generate a deposit address so you can fund a Polymarket position.

## Why there's no order placement

This is a deliberate boundary, not a missing feature. Placing or canceling an order on Polymarket requires custody of a key that can act on your behalf — exactly what Skopos's non-custodial design rules out. Read the odds, deposit if you want to, then place the actual bet yourself on Polymarket.
